# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PM hiring managers, recruiters, and interview panelists evaluating Varun for
senior product roles (Staff PM / GPM / product leadership). They arrive from a
resume link or LinkedIn, skim for 60-120 seconds on desktop or phone, and are
deciding one thing: does this person's judgment and shipping record earn a
screen? Secondary: teams in AI/data/platform he'd like to talk to.

## Product Purpose

varunb.me is Varun's portfolio: proof that he both operates at senior level in
large companies (Meta, Dropbox, Autodesk) and ships real products alone, end to
end (two apps live on the App Store). Success = a skimming hiring manager can
extract the story fast, believes it because every claim is checkable, and
reaches out. The site is also evidence of taste: it must itself be a
well-made product.

## Brand Personality

Factual, warm, rigorous. First-person, plain language, no cleverness: Varun
explicitly rejected slogan headlines ("I ship the product, not just the spec")
in favor of name-first, evidence-forward presentation. Honesty is the
signature move: status labels are never aspirational (Live / Beta / Prototype /
Passed), costs and unproven bets are stated on every case study. Voice follows
his Register A profile: chained clauses joined by so/because/which/where,
hedged precision, no em-dashes anywhere.

## Anti-references

- Squarespace/Webflow PM-portfolio template sameness (interchangeable hero +
  card grid + gradient animations). His edge is hand-built distinctiveness.
- Snarky or aphoristic headlines; "empower/seamless/leverage" copy.
- Animation showreels, chat gimmicks, showy metrics dashboards: research on
  ~250 PM portfolios showed these sell volume, not judgment.
- Fake precision: numbers without sources, aspirational status labels.
- Resume-on-a-webpage flatness (the failure mode of the plain sites in the
  same research).

## Design Principles

- Evidence over adjectives: every claim near its proof (screenshot, number,
  App Store link, named product).
- The 60-second skim is the primary user journey; depth is earned, not forced.
- Honesty is the differentiator: show what it cost, what's unproven, what was
  killed on purpose.
- Typography-led restraint; the work's screenshots supply the color.
- Nothing may rot: no build step, no external requests, verifier-gated
  publishing; a stale or broken portfolio is worse than none.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as the bar: 4.5:1 body contrast in both color schemes, descriptive
alt text on every image (verifier-enforced), semantic heading order, visible
focus states, prefers-reduced-motion honored for any motion added, light and
dark via prefers-color-scheme.
