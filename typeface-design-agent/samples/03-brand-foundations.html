Messaging rules

what we claim, and what we are not allowed to claim

Built for the second half of the run
Proof: 400-hour wet-road wear test · claim.durability.118 · approved US, CA
62% recycled upper without losing grip
Proof: GRS 4.0 certification · claim.recycled.204 · disclosure required at 11px minimum
Made for city running, not repurposed from trail
Proof: design brief · claim.positioning.031 · approved US only

Compliance guardrails

hard blocks, never scored

Never claim injury prevention or medically proven · regulated health claim Never state a price without a live PIM read · stale price blocks Never use 100% recycled · the verified figure is 62% Never place an athlete image without a signed release on file Sustainability claims require claim.recycled.204 and its disclosure

Guardrails are evaluated before rendering. A generation that trips one is discarded and recomposed, not surfaced with a warning.

Values

max 5 · what to emphasize

Endurance
We make gear for the second half of the run, not the first. Everything is judged on how it holds up at hour three.
Sustainability
Recycled materials wherever they perform. We say what a product is made of, plainly.
Community
Local run clubs come before sponsorships. We show real runners on real routes.
